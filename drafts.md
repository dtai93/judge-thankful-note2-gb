6 (shawl, ~390) Thank you. All day you have held other people's sentences across your lap, two at a time, and you have not let either one slip. 8 (letterpress, ~350) Thank you. You lower each pair onto the same soft pad and press, then you turn the sheets and press again, so the type bites evenly. Nobody's sentence is left pale. The pad keeps the faint warmth of the press, a dent that will meet the next sheet kindly.

10 (quilt, ~400) Thank you for not letting the first voice win just by arriving first.
